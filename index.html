Moduliv was created to serve people who need homes.

The housing crisis is a cost crisis. So we spent three years engineering the cost out of building: a standardised steel-framed system with fewer parts, an established supply chain, and a formalised on-site process that finishes homes faster for less. Everyone should have a high-quality home to live in and enjoy. We built a company on that principle, without debt.

    Asked and answered.

    I don't have land. Can you still help?

    Yes. It's the question we hear most. Tell us the area you want to live in and we'll help you look for a plot and work out which of our homes fits it. More on land.

    How do you achieve lower costs?

    Three years of engineering went into a standardised steel-framed system that needs fewer parts and cuts pre-build costs. An efficient supply chain delivers it to site at the lowest possible cost, and the system is designed for fast on-site assembly, which cuts time and labour.

    Do you have a factory?

    No, on purpose. Moduliv works with established fabrication and manufacturing partners across the UK. That keeps us asset-light and keeps your costs down.

    What kind of homes do you build?

    Single-family homes and low-rise apartments, from a 40 m² one-bed to a 112 m² four-bed house. All designed to Nationally Described Space Standards.

    What about quality and certification?

    Every home is SCI / NHBC (Stage 1 and 2) certified, BOPAS certified, Homes England grant approved and pre-approved by the main mortgage lenders.

    How energy efficient are they?

    A-rated as standard. Factory quality control means the insulation and airtightness you were promised are the insulation and airtightness you get.

    Who do you sell to?

    Wind and water-tight buildings to contractors and developers, and finished homes to home buyers through LIV. Homes.

    Where do you build?

    Across the UK, with our showcase module open to visit in Preston, Lancashire.
